Converting scores to NCLC levels

Tables published by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). The green row marks the NCLC 5 threshold — the minimum target for several francophone immigration programs. (NCLC is the French-language equivalent of CLB.)

TEF Canada tests taken after December 10, 2023

This scale only applies to tests taken after December 10, 2023. If you took the TEF between October 1, 2019 and December 10, 2023, the previous scale applies (NCLC 5 = 350–399 in all four skills).

TCF Canada current scale

Reading and listening: score on a 331–699 scale. Writing and speaking: mark from 0 to 20. For NCLC 5, you need exactly 6/20 minimum in each production skill.

What this means in practice for CLB 5: on the TCF, the step between 5/20 (NCLC 4) and 6/20 (NCLC 5) often comes down to fully completing the task and hitting the word count. On the TEF (new scale), the production thresholds are 330 for writing and 387 for speaking — speaking demands proportionally more than writing.

Official source: canada.ca — Get your language assessed. Always check the official page before submitting an application: requirements vary by program.
