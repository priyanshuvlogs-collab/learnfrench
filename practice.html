Task bank

Train under real exam conditions

Each task reproduces the official format. Start the timer, respect the word count, then check your work against the CLB 5 checklist before moving on to the self-assessment.

Note: the task prompts are in French, exactly as they will be on exam day. Reading and understanding a French prompt is part of the test — don't practice with translations.

For speaking tasks: record yourself with your phone, then listen back while following the checklist. Count your hesitations: at CLB 5, your pace must stay steady, even with simple sentences.
