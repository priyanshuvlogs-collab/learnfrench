Listening & Reading

Mock tests with instant CLB / NCLC estimation

Auto-graded mini-mocks in the official MCQ format (4 options, one correct answer, progressive difficulty). Listening audio is generated in French by your browser and each recording can be played at most twice, as on the real exams. Your raw score is converted to an estimated scaled score and NCLC level.

Questions and answer options are in French, exactly as on exam day. For best listening audio, use Chrome or Edge (they include a French voice).

Full-length practice

Official mock tests available for free

Once you pass the mini-mocks consistently, do at least one full-length official practice test per skill before exam day. These are the free ones published or endorsed by the exam providers.

TEF Canada  Official resources

  • Le français des affaires — sample test papers: official example items for all four TEF skills, from the exam provider (CCI Paris Île-de-France).
  • TEF Canada official page: current exam structure (listening 60 questions/40 min, reading 50 questions/60 min) and test-day rules.
  • RFI Savoirs: free graded French listening exercises based on real radio — ideal daily training for the listening section.

TCF Canada  Official resources

  • France Éducation international — TCF Canada: the exam provider's official page, with the candidate handbook and sample tasks (listening and reading: 39 MCQs each).
  • TV5Monde — Apprendre le français: free "TCF practice" section with timed listening and reading question series by level.
  • RFI Savoirs: authentic French audio with comprehension quizzes, close to the TCF listening item style.
How to use mock tests efficiently: take a mock, review every wrong answer (for listening, re-read the transcript and find the exact sentence you missed), wait a few hours, then retake it. Move to a full official mock only when you score at least 75% twice in a row here.
